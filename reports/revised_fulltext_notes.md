## Full text read

**Golakiya, Chauhan, Bari & Dhamaniya (2022), “Pedestrian safety analysis at urban midblock section under mixed traffic conditions using time to collision as surrogate safety measure,” _Current Science_ 123(9), 1117–1128.** DOI: [10.18520/cs/v123/i9/1117-1128](https://doi.org/10.18520/cs/v123/i9/1117-1128). Full text: [publisher PDF](https://www.currentscience.ac.in/Volumes/123/09/1117.pdf); publisher returned HTTP 200 and `application/pdf` on 2026-10-25. Also read the entire 12-page local copy `data/raw/public_sources/peer_reviewed_india_midblock_ttc.pdf` with `pdftotext -layout` (65,955 extracted characters). SHA-256 of local PDF: `cd7a8af7e49f59b0f4f0ae5204953ba3252f0e68ec75a3ac1f409788b37f7a33`.

- **Problem/data:** micro-level pedestrian–vehicle conflict safety at four uncontrolled urban midblock sites in Surat, Vadodara, Ahmedabad, and Jaipur. Video observations were collected 7:00–19:00 on a dry-weather day; trajectories were manually extracted from video.
- **Measures/method:** TTC as a surrogate safety measure, analyzed separately for pedestrian-pass-first and vehicle-pass-first crossing sequences. The study examines gender and vehicle category; fits TTC regression models with a generalized linear mixed model approach; and uses clustering on TTC thresholds to define safety levels.
- **Validation/scope:** empirical observations support the fitted models and proposed thresholds for the studied sites/conditions. This is not crash-outcome validation, causal treatment evaluation, or a site/package prioritization framework. The four-city Indian context is relevant background, not evidence about IIIT Kottayam.
