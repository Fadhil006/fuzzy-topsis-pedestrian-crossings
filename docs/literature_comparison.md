This project's measured-range preprocessing and crisp weighted ideals are documented adaptations. |

The project question is whether adding a verified essential-destination access criterion changes the relative ordering or top group of feasible crossing-upgrade packages after the baseline criteria have been held fixed. A ranking change alone does not establish improved decisions; independent review is required.
