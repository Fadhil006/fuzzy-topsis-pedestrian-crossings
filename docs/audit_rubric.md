# Provisional audit-rating rubric

Use this rubric only after a local road/accessibility expert has reviewed the items and field assessors have practised on the same examples. Component evidence must be retained; the overall rating alone is insufficient.

| Score | Accessibility deficiency | Control or visibility deficiency | Essential-destination access importance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | No barrier observed in inspected approach features | No visible deficiency in inspected features | No verified essential destination depends on this crossing within the declared study catchment |
| 1 | Localized impediment; crossing remains usable via a short accessible route | Minor wear or partial obstruction documented | A verified destination is served, with a practical alternate route |
| 2 | Material barrier or difficult detour; independent use may be constrained | Material marking, sight, lighting or control concern recorded for engineering review | Crossing materially shortens access to one essential destination or route |
| 3 | Observed approach is impassable for a mobility-device user or has no usable connection | Severe obstruction or missing/failed visible facility requiring prompt engineering inspection | Crossing is a key route to multiple verified essential destinations with no practical alternative |

These are operational categories, not statements of legal compliance or measured crash risk. A trained engineer must decide whether controls are appropriate for traffic speed, volume, geometry, and local standards. Assessors record the evidence and uncertainty from disagreement. The destination catchment and meaning of a practical alternate route must be defined before data collection and applied consistently.
