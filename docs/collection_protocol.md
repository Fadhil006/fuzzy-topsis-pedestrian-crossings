# Proposed field collection and validation protocol

## Boundary and alternatives

Confirm the IIIT Kottayam campus boundary and the immediately adjoining public-road approaches on site and with the responsible authorities. Walk the boundary, assign a crossing ID to each verified marked or routinely used crossing, record GPS coordinates and approach descriptions, and photograph infrastructure without exposing identifiable pedestrians. Make one feasible intervention package per crossing. Specify the action, which deficiency it addresses, dependencies, and why an authority would consider it feasible. Record rejected candidates with reasons.

## Counts

On two ordinary weekdays, observe morning, midday and evening periods. In each period count at least three separate 15-minute intervals for both crossing pedestrians and vehicles on the same approach. Preserve every raw count, interval duration, weather and event note in count_intervals_template.csv. Convert each count to an hourly rate by multiplying by 60/minutes. Collate the minimum, median and maximum hourly rates into the intervention row. This range describes observed temporal variation, not a confidence interval. If an event, rain or traffic disruption makes a session atypical, flag it and repeat or analyse it separately rather than silently discarding it.

## Paired audit

Before observing sites, agree on photo-supported descriptions of the 0–3 levels for accessibility and control/visibility. Two assessors independently score ramp/curb transition, tactile information, surface and path continuity, marking, lighting, sight obstruction and crossing-control appropriateness. Record component observations and evidence IDs; use the 0–3 overall severity rating only after those observations. Retain both individual ratings. An adjudicated central rating must have a written reason. Use the smaller/larger assessor rating as low/high where disagreement remains. The proposed scales require comparison against applicable local standards and engineer review; they are not a substitute for a compliance audit.

## Extension

List essential destinations (for example, verified health care, transit or campus services) and define a walk-network catchment before ratings are collected. Give E1 a 0–3 access-importance rating based on whether the crossing is a necessary link and the practical availability of an alternative route. Document destination IDs and path evidence. Do not translate raw nearby-destination counts directly into E1 if that merely duplicates pedestrian demand. Investigate correlation with demand and report any overlap.

## Cleaning and independent review

Require unique alternative and crossing IDs, consistent units and survey dates, ordered fuzzy ranges, photo IDs, and written feasibility status. Keep missing values missing until re-observation; do not impute zero. Preserve excluded candidate rows in the preprocessing log. A separate reviewer should inspect a sample of sites or evidence without seeing the model ranking, then record agreement and disagreements. No such review has yet occurred.
